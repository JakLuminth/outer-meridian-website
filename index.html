PEOPLE. PLACES. FURTHER TOGETHER.

Space is the setting.
The crew is the story.

Outer Meridian follows the rhythms of a civilian exploration crew: preparing for an outing, gathering supplies, getting to know your crewmates, and sharing a meal back aboard.

The prototype brings those small moments into a persistent, cooperative world. Play in third person, work alongside NPC crew, and return to a ship that remembers your progress.

01

Live aboard

Care for your officer’s needs, spend time with the crew, and find your place in the daily life of the ship.

02

Head out together

Travel between ship, station, and a planetary clearing. Bring a field kit, gather ingredients, and bring something home.

03

Make it last

Shared progress and saved characters carry across sessions. Persistent play is part of that foundation.

THE JOURNEY SO FAR

A playable foundation.
A bigger horizon.

Today’s build is an early prototype with a short playable outing, crew interactions, and persistent multiplayer. Art, animation, usability, and hosting are still being developed.

Our visual goal is photorealism with a practical, lived-in civilian character: warm light, useful spaces, and people who belong in them. The concept above shows that direction; it is not a screenshot of the current game.
